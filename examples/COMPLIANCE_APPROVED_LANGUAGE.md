# COMPLIANCE-APPROVED LANGUAGE

## SAFE PHRASES TO USE:

### Qualification Language:
✅ "See if you qualify"
✅ "May qualify for up to $X"
✅ "Could be eligible for"
✅ "Answer questions to check eligibility"
✅ "Find out what you qualify for"

❌ "You're guaranteed approval"
❌ "Everyone qualifies"
❌ "You're already approved"

---

### Benefit Amounts:
✅ "Up to $25,000" (not "get $25,000")
✅ "Could grow to $2M+" (not "will grow")
✅ "Potential for tax-free growth"
✅ "May provide $X benefit"

❌ Guaranteed specific dollar amounts
❌ "You will get $X"
❌ Promises of specific returns

---

### Health/Underwriting:
✅ "Answer health questions"
✅ "May require medical information"
✅ "Based on health and age"
✅ "Subject to underwriting approval"

❌ "No health questions"
❌ "Guaranteed acceptance"
❌ "No medical exam required" (unless true for specific product)

---

### Policy Features:
✅ "Tax-free growth potential"
✅ "Access cash value tax-free" (if IUL/Whole Life)
✅ "Death benefit paid to beneficiaries"
✅ "No contribution limits" (if true for IUL)

❌ "Guaranteed returns"
❌ "Never lose money" (even with floor)
❌ "Better than 401k" (comparative claims)

---

### Urgency Language:
✅ "Rates may change"
✅ "Limited time offer"
✅ "Availability subject to change"
✅ "Act soon"

❌ "This is your last chance ever"
❌ "Only 3 spots left" (false scarcity)
❌ "Offer expires in 1 hour" (unless true)

---

## PRODUCT-SPECIFIC GUIDELINES:

### Final Expense:
✅ "Helps cover funeral and burial costs"
✅ "Designed for seniors aged 50-85"
✅ "May qualify with pre-existing conditions"
✅ "Benefits paid to beneficiaries"

❌ "Pays all funeral costs"
❌ "No health questions asked"
❌ "Everyone over 50 qualifies"

---

### IUL (Index Universal Life):
✅ "Potential for tax-free growth based on market index"
✅ "Cash value can be accessed tax-free via loans"
✅ "Subject to policy charges and fees"
✅ "Returns linked to market index performance"

❌ "Stock market gains without risk"
❌ "Guaranteed market returns"
❌ "Always grows tax-free" (without mentioning loans/distributions)

---

### Term Life:
✅ "Coverage for a specific term (10, 20, 30 years)"
✅ "Affordable protection for families"
✅ "Death benefit if insured dies during term"
✅ "May be renewable or convertible"

❌ "Cheapest life insurance"
❌ "Coverage forever"
❌ "Never expires" (term does expire)

---

## REQUIRED DISCLAIMERS (Where Applicable):

### General:
- "Benefits subject to eligibility and approval"
- "Rates vary based on age, health, and other factors"
- "Policy details vary by state"
- "Consult with licensed agent for details"

### Investment-Related (IUL):
- "Cash value growth not guaranteed"
- "Policy loans and withdrawals may reduce death benefit"
- "Surrender charges may apply"
- "Market index changes don't guarantee cash value growth"

---

## WHAT TO AVOID COMPLETELY:

❌ **Medical Advice:**
- Don't mention specific medical conditions as qualifying factors
- Don't suggest life insurance replaces medical insurance

❌ **Investment Advice:**
- Don't compare to specific investment vehicles unless factual
- Don't promise specific returns

❌ **Legal/Tax Advice:**
- Use "may provide tax benefits" not "eliminates all taxes"
- Use "consult tax advisor" for specific tax situations

❌ **False Scarcity:**
- Don't create fake urgency with made-up deadlines
- Don't claim "last chance" unless truly final opportunity

❌ **Comparative Claims Without Proof:**
- Don't say "best life insurance"
- Don't claim superiority over competitors without data

---

## SAFE CALL-TO-ACTION LANGUAGE:

✅ "Click to see if you qualify"
✅ "Answer 2 questions to check eligibility"
✅ "Get a free quote"
✅ "Find out your options"
✅ "Speak with licensed agent"

❌ "Get approved now"
❌ "Claim your policy"
❌ "Activate your benefit"

---

## TONE GUIDELINES:

### Acceptable:
- Urgent but honest
- Informative and educational
- Empathetic to pain points
- Encouraging action

### Avoid:
- Fear-mongering
- Deceptive urgency
- Overpromising
- Manipulative language


---

## BEST PRACTICES:

1. **Be truthful** - Every claim must be accurate
2. **Be specific** - "May qualify for up to $X" not "huge benefits"
3. **Include context** - "Tax-free via loans" not just "tax-free"
4. **Avoid absolutes** - "May", "could", "potential" instead of "will", "guaranteed"
5. **Disclose limitations** - Fees, charges, restrictions
6. **Encourage consultation** - "Speak with licensed agent" for details
