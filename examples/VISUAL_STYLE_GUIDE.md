# VISUAL STYLE GUIDE - BASED ON PROVEN WINNERS

## CAPTION STYLE (Pop-Style, Breaking News Aesthetic):

### RED BANNERS (Full-Width):
**When to use:** Dollar amounts, deadlines, shocking stats, warnings

**Examples:**
- "SENIORS ARE CLAIMING $25,000"
- "PAYING 40% IN TAXES?"
- "DEADLINE: DEC 31ST"
- "LAST CHANCE"

**Placement:** Top of frame only, full width (never bottom)
**Font:** Bold, all caps, white text on red background
**Size:** Large — prominent and impossible to miss on mobile

---

### YELLOW WARNINGS (⚠️ Symbols):
**When to use:** Opening urgency, time-sensitive messaging, breaking news

**Examples:**
- "⚠️ LAST CHANCE ⚠️"
- "⚠️ BREAKING NEWS ⚠️"
- "⚠️ THIS WAS BURIED ⚠️"

**Placement:** Top of frame, centered
**Font:** Bold, all caps, black text with yellow background or yellow warning symbols
**Animation:** Can pulse or fade in for emphasis

---

### GREEN/PINK HIGHLIGHTS (Inline Text):
**When to use:** Trust-building phrases, friction removers, social proof quotes

**Examples:**
- "no catch" (highlighted in green)
- "I didn't believe it either" (highlighted in pink)
- "no calls" (highlighted in green)
- "perfectly legal" (highlighted in green)
- "no spam" (highlighted in green)

**Placement:** Appears inline with voiceover, highlighting key phrases
**Font:** Bold, lowercase or title case
**Style:** Colored highlight behind white text

---

### WHITE/BOLD TEXT (Standard Captions):
**When to use:** Product names, key benefits, CTAs, general emphasis

**Examples:**
- "TAX-FREE WEALTH"
- "CLICK BELOW ↓"
- "IUL POLICY"

**Placement:** Upper third of frame (never lower third)
**Font:** Bold, all caps or title case — bigger and more prominent than feels comfortable
**Color:** White text with black outline/shadow for readability

---

## MOTION GRAPHICS:

### Opening Sequence (0-3 seconds):
**"BREAKING NEWS" Graphic:**
- Fake news channel aesthetic (red/blue graphics)
- Bold "BREAKING NEWS" text
- Can include lower third news ticker
- Creates immediate urgency and authority
- Duration: 2-3 seconds max

---

### Text Overlays During Social Proof (15-30 seconds):
**Appearing Text Bubbles:**
- Social proof quotes appear as text bubbles
- Examples: "I thought it was a scam", "I qualified in 60s"
- Animate in from sides or fade in
- Stay on screen 2-3 seconds each
- White text on semi-transparent dark backgrounds

---

### Benefit Display (30-45 seconds):
**Animated Bullet Points:**
- Display 3-4 key benefits as animated text
- Appear one by one as voiceover mentions them
- Examples: "$25,000 benefit" → "No loan" → "No catch"
- Can use checkmarks or icons
- Clean, professional animation

---

### End Card CTA (55-60 seconds):
**Pulsing Button + Arrow:**
- Animated "CLICK BELOW" button that pulses
- Downward arrow pointing to CTA
- Can animate in from bottom
- Bold, high contrast (white on dark or colored background)

---

## B-ROLL STRATEGY:

### Clip Duration Rules:
- **Each B-roll clip:** 1-3 seconds max — punchy cuts, not lingering shots
- **Full-screen B-roll:** 2-3 seconds absolute max, then cut back to speaker
- Never use long, slow stock footage — if it feels like a Getty Images ad, it's wrong
- B-roll must be attention-grabbing and authentic, not generic

### 9:16 Layout — Split-Screen (Preferred):
- **Bottom half:** Speaker/talking head (always visible)
- **Top half:** Rapid B-roll clips (1-3s each) relevant to what's being said
- This keeps the speaker connected while reinforcing the message visually
- Use full-screen B-roll only for maximum-impact moments (shocking stat, key reveal)

---

### Match Audience Environment:

**Seniors (Final Expense):**
- Warm home settings
- Family gatherings (grandparents with grandchildren)
- Comfortable living rooms
- Peaceful retirement scenes
- Emotional: comforting, family-focused

**Business Owners (IUL):**
- Modern offices
- Professional settings
- Tax documents/spreadsheets
- Success imagery (nice cars, homes)
- Emotional: frustrated with taxes → relief

**Truckers (IUL):**
- Truck stops
- Semi trucks on highways
- Diesel pumps
- Trucker in cab
- Emotional: working-class, authentic, rugged

**Young Families (Term):**
- Home settings
- Kids playing
- Family activities
- Parents with children
- Emotional: protective, loving, concerned

---

### Emotional Arc in B-Roll:

**Problem Section (5-15s):**
- Show audience experiencing pain
- Examples: Stressed with tax bills, worried parent, senior alone

**Social Proof (15-30s):**
- Show relief, success, happiness
- Examples: Family smiling, relaxed business owner, confident trucker

**Solution (30-45s):**
- Show aspirational but relatable success
- Examples: Financial freedom imagery, family protected, peace of mind

---

## COLOR PSYCHOLOGY BY PRODUCT:

### Final Expense:
- **Primary:** Red (urgency), warm earth tones
- **Accent:** White (simplicity), cream (comfort)
- **Mood:** Warm, comforting, urgent
- **Avoid:** Cold blues, aggressive colors

### IUL (Business Owners):
- **Primary:** Yellow (attention), blue (credibility)
- **Accent:** White (clean), earth tones (grounded)
- **Mood:** Professional, urgent, authoritative
- **Avoid:** Overly bright or playful colors

### IUL (Working Class):
- **Primary:** Earth tones, yellow warnings
- **Accent:** Blue (trust), white (clarity)
- **Mood:** Authentic, no-BS, relatable
- **Avoid:** Corporate or overly polished aesthetics

### Term Life:
- **Primary:** Blue/green (protection, family)
- **Accent:** Warm tones (love, care)
- **Mood:** Protective, caring, urgent
- **Avoid:** Dark or ominous colors

---

## PLATFORM-SPECIFIC FORMATTING:

### Mobile (Primary Platform):
- Vertical 9:16 format
- Large text (readable on small screens)
- High contrast
- Minimal clutter
- Captions in upper third only (never lower third, never center)

### Desktop:
- Same vertical format works
- Can add slightly more detail in backgrounds
- Text can be marginally smaller

---

## BEST PRACTICES:

1. **Captions always visible** - Never rely on audio alone
2. **High contrast** - White on dark, dark on light, colored banners
3. **Animation serves purpose** - Don't animate just to animate
4. **Breaking news aesthetic** - Creates urgency and authority
5. **Emotion through color** - Match colors to emotional journey
6. **B-roll matches audience** - Authentic to their lifestyle, never generic stock
7. **B-roll is punchy** - 1-3s per clip, full-screen max 2-3s
8. **9:16 split-screen** - Speaker bottom half, B-roll top half (preferred layout)
9. **Mobile-first design** - Most views are mobile
10. **Less is more** - Don't over-caption or over-animate
